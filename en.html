Korea Cultural Art Passionate youth

KCAP — a non-profit association of young Korean artists. “Together, further!”

AboutKCAP

Founded in 2018 and licensed by Jeollanam-do Province (No. 326), KCAP brings together around 300 artists in their 20s and 30s — painters, sculptors, ceramicists, media and installation artists.

Since 2021 it has run curated shows and art fairs featuring 30 artists and 317 works, covered in 50 press reports.
