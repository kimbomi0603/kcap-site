Korea Cultural Art Passionate youth

KCAP — a non-profit incorporated association of young Korean artists. “Together, further!”

About

Who we are

KCAP was founded in 2018 and licensed by Jeollanam-do Province as a non-profit incorporated association (No. 326). Around 300 artists in their 20s and 30s — painters, sculptors, ceramicists, media and installation artists — work together through KCAP.

We support young artists' creative work and rights, and aim to be a home where regional young artists can keep creating without leaving their communities.

Our mission areas: arts education and hands-on learning; cultural projects for national and regional development; information, promotion and archiving for artists.
